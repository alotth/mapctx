      ## Expected Outcome
      The mapctx-traycer skill documents multi-task orchestration, one-child-per-task assignment, fresh-child handoff for contaminated or context-limited sessions, planner wave ordering, and the Traycer/MapCtx ownership boundary.

      - [x] Guidance preserves claim, dispatch, receipt, worktree, dependency, and ownership rules.
      - [x] Slice remains documentation-only and states that live agent creation is still a Traycer boundary.
