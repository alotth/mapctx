  - summary: Distribute paid plan periods across account-linked runs and show allocated cost per task and epic.
  - description: |
      ## Product Context

      Codex and Claude Code subscription runs have zero per-run billed cash. Users still need each task and epic to show its share of the fixed plan payment.

      ## Expected Outcome

      - [ ] Use existing Account and PlanPeriod manual entry for actual amount and period; bind runs to the account and billing route that paid for them.
      - [ ] Allocate the period fee across priced runs in all projects bound to that account, proportional to shadow cost, with deterministic rounding and no double count.
      - [ ] Mark open-period shares provisional, recompute safely as usage arrives, and freeze or version the allocation when the period closes.
      - [ ] Keep runs without priced usage unattributed; show billed cash, shadow, and allocated fee separately per task and epic.
      - [ ] Permit optional billing import to populate plan periods when a reliable bill is available; never infer the paid amount from public subscription prices.
      - [ ] Test cross-project denominator, retries, late usage, zero-usage periods, and task/epic budget consumption.
