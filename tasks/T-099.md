  - description: |
      ## Product Context

      Planned, forecast and measured time are three disconnected models. `estimatedEffort` is free text that nothing reads; the Gantt "Planned" bar only comes from `startDate`/`dueDate` (7 of 99 non-epic tasks); the forecast uses `workload` (null silently defaults to Normal) plus receipts. Result: most tasks show no planned, some show planned without actual, and planned-vs-actual cannot be measured. 34 of 43 done tasks have no receipt.

      ## Time convention (decided 2026-09-30, operator)

      `estimatedEffort` from now on means **agent active time** (`activeTime`, ADR 0003 time model: sum of inter-event gaps under the idle threshold), the same unit the forecast reads. Human waiting time (agent blocked on an answer, review queue, idle sessions) is NOT part of it; it is tracked separately as `leadTime` (readyAt -> doneAt) and wall clock. Legacy estimates on existing tasks were authored as human working days (T-065 measured ~28x over-estimate); they are not reinterpreted, only new/claimed tasks follow the new convention, and legacy values are flagged `legacy-human` when compared.

      Measuring agent active time vs human/parked time from session transcripts (receipt intervals, `activeTimeCoverage: measured`, four-figure Gantt) is scoped to T-100, which depends on this task. Until T-100 lands, every active time stays `substituted` and must be shown as such, never as measured.
